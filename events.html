Book Haven Bookstore is a gathering place as much as a shop. These are the events, partnerships and readers that make up our community.

Student Study Evenings

Ongoing

Community Book Drive

Donate gently used books to our collection bin and we pass them on to the Rivertown Community Library.

Community Involvement

Rivertown Schools Reading Partnership

Our booksellers volunteer each week to read with primary-school classes, and we donate a classroom set of books every semester.

Local Author Shelf

We reserve a permanent display near the entrance for writers living in the county, and we charge no consignment fee.
